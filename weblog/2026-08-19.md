I have a usb wifi adapter (TP-Link Archer T2U Plus [RTL8821AU]) that I use with the T60, and fortunately Haiku had the drivers already! Apparently the wifi drivers are mostly from OpenBSD/FreeBSD and Haiku borrows them through some kind of compatibility layer.
