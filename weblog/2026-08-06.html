I would really like to read Clarice Lispector at some point. I've seen her name mentioned twice by two different people on the internet who seem interesting to me. From excerpts and descriptions, her writing seems so pure and strong. The kind of writing that reminds me how words together are more than their basic meanings, and reading is more like tickling our senses to provoke some richer, half-spontaneous dream (to dream someone else's dream!).

The excerpt I heard was in a video reading the first two paragraphs of Lispector's Agua Vida.
